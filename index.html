emu8086-web — Online 8086 Assembler, Emulator & Debugger

emu8086-web is a free, browser-based Intel 8086 assembler, emulator and debugger inspired by the EMU8086 workflow. Write 8086 assembly, assemble it and step through it instruction by instruction, while watching the registers, flags, memory, stack and an 80×25 text screen. Nothing is installed: everything runs in your browser.
